Autonomy · UofT

Recoverability-Aware World Models

Prediction-only world models can call a scene “safe” after the point of no return. This objective keeps whether the car can still recover, then distils it into a driving policy.

  1. 01Mine unrecoverable scenes in headless CARLA
  2. 02Generate counterfactual variants with NVIDIA Cosmos
  3. 03Train a recoverability-aware world model
  4. 04Distil it into a DAgger-trained driving policy

Pipeline

  • CARLA scene mining
  • ↓ Cosmos counterfactuals
  • ↓ recovery labels
  • ↓ world model
  • ↓ DAgger policy

PyTorch · CARLA · NVIDIA Cosmos · imitation learning

UBC CPSC 440

with Jerry Gai

Unsupervised Patient Stratification

Routine Chinese health checkups mix structured labs with free-text organ reports. A VAE compresses 18,572 sparse features into a 32-dimensional latent space; K-Means on that space recovers seven clinical phenotypes, including sensorineural hearing loss, liver fibrosis and hypothyroidism, with no labels at all.

Code and report ↗

Pipeline

  • Labs + free-text reports
  • ↓ VAE, 32-d latent space
  • ↓ K-Means
  • ↓ 7 clinical phenotypes

VAE · GMVAE · K-Means · UMAP

Fig. 1 UMAP of the VAE latent space, 15,000 training visits. Each phenotype occupies its own region; the healthy baseline sits apart on the right.

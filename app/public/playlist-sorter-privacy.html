playlist-sorter privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

playlist-sorter is a personal tool used only by its owner, Frederick Wang. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

  • It reads the owner's YouTube subscriptions, channel uploads and playlists.
  • It adds videos to the owner's own playlists. It never deletes videos, playlists or anything else.

What it stores and shares

  • No data is collected, sold or shared with anyone.
  • The Google access token is stored only on the owner's own computer, in the operating system's credential store.
  • A local list of already-filed video IDs is kept on the same computer to avoid duplicates.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
